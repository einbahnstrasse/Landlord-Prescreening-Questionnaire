Before we schedule a tour

The evaluation process is not one‑sided.

As prospective tenants, we recognize that landlords must evaluate applicants before entering a lease. We believe it is equally reasonable for tenants to evaluate the landlord, the property, and the management practices before investing further time. Just as landlords assess prospective tenants, tenants assess prospective landlords.

Please provide brief, candid responses below. Completion does not guarantee further consideration; we reserve the right to discontinue at any point. Your answers help us decide whether to proceed to a tour.
